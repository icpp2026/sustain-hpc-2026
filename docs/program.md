## Workshop Program

SUSTAIN-HPC 2026 is organized as a **half-day workshop** combining invited keynote talks, peer-reviewed paper presentations, and short work-in-progress talks. Rather than maximizing the number of presentations, the workshop prioritizes technical depth, interaction, and community building.

### Tentative Half-Day Program

| Session | Duration |
|---------|----------|
| Opening remarks and workshop overview | 10 minutes |
| Invited keynote talks | 60 minutes |
| Peer-reviewed paper session I | 60 minutes |
| Short paper / work-in-progress session | 30 minutes |
| Break | 15 minutes |
| Peer-reviewed paper session II | 40 minutes |
| Closing discussion | 10 minutes |

!!! note
    The final program will be adjusted based on the number and quality of accepted submissions and will be published here before the workshop.

### Presentation Formats

The workshop includes the following presentation formats:

- Invited keynote talks
- Peer-reviewed full paper presentations
- Short paper and work-in-progress lightning talks

### Invited Speakers

Invited speakers will provide complementary perspectives across sustainable HPC, distributed systems, architecture, emerging computing technologies, and infrastructure operation. The confirmed speaker list will be announced on this page.
